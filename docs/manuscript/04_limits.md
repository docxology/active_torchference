## Scope and limitations

The tests and diagnostic plots exercise particular synthetic fixtures.
The continuous likelihood uses the posterior mean, and the rollout
uncertainty is an approximation. The planning routine refines a sampled
action distribution rather than solving an arbitrary sequential
optimization problem.

Archived episode summaries provide evidence of the values the software
recorded. Fresh validation shows that the current implementation meets the
selected numerical checks on CPU. Generalization to new environments,
performance across devices, and scientific claims about biological cognition
require separate studies.
